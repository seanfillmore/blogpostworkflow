# Visual Hierarchy And Clarity

## Enforce a strict visual hierarchy — headline first (it does the targeting), then product/key visual, then supporting elements.

**Why it works:** The eye needs one deliberate entry point; when everything competes for attention nothing is read, so the headline must own the focal point and everything else must recede.

**Evidence offered:** Creator shows a rejected strategist ad with no focal point versus a compliant one; assertion otherwise.

**Fit here (7/10):** Durable design principle, not platform mechanics. Real Skin Care's highest-leverage image real estate is the Amazon listing image stack ($1,800/mo channel) and the Shopify PDP — both editable by a solo operator with Canva, no designer required.

*Source: Dara Denney — "AI Static Ads Masterclass (FULL GUIDE)" (5C5VhqW9HCc)*

## Run a one-second comprehension test: if a stranger can't tell what you're selling instantly, kill the creative — clarity beats creativity.

**Why it works:** Comprehension is the gate before persuasion; ambiguity spends the only second of attention you get, so unclear assets fail before the message is even evaluated.

**Evidence offered:** Creator applies it live to reject a strategist's ad; assertion otherwise.

**Fit here (8/10):** Durable principle, free, and testable by one person in minutes. It is a CRO-stage screen — exactly the gate that precedes any paid spend under the required sequence — and applies to Amazon main images, PDP heroes, and email headers alike.
