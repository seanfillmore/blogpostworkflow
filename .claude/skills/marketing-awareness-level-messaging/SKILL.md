# Awareness Level Messaging

## Give every creative exactly one job — one goal, one persona, one objection — never two.

**Why it works:** An asset that tries to educate, sell an offer, and answer objections at once loses the focal point and speaks clearly to nobody, so comprehension and click intent both drop.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion from experience; creator says this is where she most often sees statics fail.

**Fit here (7/10):** Durable principle class — transfers straight to Amazon listing image slots, Shopify PDP hero blocks, and single-purpose emails, all of which a solo operator controls for free. Directly supports the CRO stage that gates everything else.

*Source: Dara Denney — "AI Static Ads Masterclass (FULL GUIDE)" (5C5VhqW9HCc)*

## Pick a narrow persona — stage and active objection, not a broad demographic — before writing any copy.

**Why it works:** Persona determines vocabulary, the type of proof required, and which objections must be answered; without it copy defaults to generic language that self-selects nobody.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion only; illustrated with the 'small business owners' counter-example.

**Fit here (7/10):** Durable positioning principle, platform-agnostic and zero-cost. For 12 SKUs across natural deodorant, oral care, and lip balm, objection-specific copy (does it actually work, detox period, aluminum-free skepticism) is usable on the PDP and Amazon bullets today without any ad spend.

*Source: Dara Denney — "AI Static Ads Masterclass (FULL GUIDE)" (5C5VhqW9HCc)*

## Match each creative to a specific level of market awareness; awareness is the bridge between persona/goal and the copy framework.

**Why it works:** Unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, and product-aware prospects need structurally different openings; mismatching the entry point means the message never lands regardless of design quality.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion; reinforced by the competitor gap analysis showing awareness levels not being targeted.

**Fit here (7/10):** Durable principle class — Schwartz-era logic that ages slowly. Applies to organic content, email sequences, and listing copy, none of which require budget or attribution. Problem-aware framing is the natural entry for natural-deodorant switchers.
