# Conversion Copy Angles

## Be concretely specific: name the demographic, use exact numbers, and lead with the time, cost, or effort outcome (e.g. '5 minutes, under $5', '2 inches in one use').

**Why it works:** Specific numbers are cognitively credible and self-verifying, while generic benefit language is skipped because it makes no falsifiable promise.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion from pattern recognition across winning creative.

**Fit here (8/10):** Durable copy principle with zero dependency on budget, list size, or attribution. Directly rewritable into PDP headlines, Amazon bullets, and email subject lines — e.g. quantified claims about hours of odor protection or transition period length.

*Source: Dara Denney — "AI Static Ads Masterclass (FULL GUIDE)" (5C5VhqW9HCc)*

## Call out your audience by name in the copy — self-selection is a targeting mechanic.

**Why it works:** Naming the reader makes them stop and identify, doing targeting work inside the creative rather than relying on the platform's audience settings.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion only.

**Fit here (6/10):** Durable copy principle; works without ads on listing copy and email. Scored below the specificity tactic because its stated payoff — replacing platform targeting — is an ad-account benefit Real Skin Care does not currently collect.

*Source: Dara Denney — "AI Static Ads Masterclass (FULL GUIDE)" (5C5VhqW9HCc)*

## Lean into the taboo — say the thing competitors are too polite to say.

**Why it works:** Naming the embarrassing reality both stops the scroll and signals that you understand the customer's actual lived problem, creating identification competitors cannot buy.

**Evidence offered:** Creator's experience; demonstrated live with the visceral 'your deodorant is ruining your shirts' pit-stain concept for Dr. Squatch.

**Fit here (8/10):** Durable persuasion principle, and the worked example in the video is literally deodorant — pit stains, odor, the natural-deodorant detox period. Free to apply to PDP copy, email, and organic content by a solo operator with no budget.

*Source: Dara Denney — "AI Static Ads Masterclass (FULL GUIDE)" (5C5VhqW9HCc)*

## Write to a primal desire (status, sex, belonging, safety, approval) and treat the product as merely the mechanism.

**Why it works:** Product benefits are rational and comparable; primal desires are the actual purchase driver, so the desire belongs in the headline and the feature belongs in the supporting copy.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion from pattern recognition across winning creative.

**Fit here (7/10):** Durable positioning principle, age-irrelevant. Usable immediately in listing and email copy; for natural body care the safety/approval axis (what you're not putting on your body, not smelling in a meeting) is directly available without spend.

*Source: Dara Denney — "AI Static Ads Masterclass (FULL GUIDE)" (5C5VhqW9HCc)*

## Open a curiosity loop — show the setup, hide the payoff behind the click — and make sure the landing page closes the loop.

**Why it works:** An unresolved gap compels the click; resolving it on-page prevents the bounce that an unpaid-off tease would cause.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion; creator notes she checks that top performers have the payoff on the landing page.

**Fit here (5/10):** Durable principle, but its payoff is measured in click-through, and Real Skin Care's binding constraint is retention, not traffic. Still usable on email subject lines at current list scale, so it earns a place inside the copy-angles skill rather than its own.

*Source: Dara Denney — "AI Static Ads Masterclass (FULL GUIDE)" (5C5VhqW9HCc)*

## Use negative framing — name explicitly what the audience is afraid of.

**Why it works:** Loss and fear are stronger motivators than gain; naming the feared outcome creates recognition and urgency that a positive benefit statement does not.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion only.

**Fit here (6/10):** Durable persuasion principle, free to apply. Overlaps heavily with the taboo angle, so it belongs as a variant inside the same copy-angles skill rather than a separate skill — duplicate skills degrade triggering.
