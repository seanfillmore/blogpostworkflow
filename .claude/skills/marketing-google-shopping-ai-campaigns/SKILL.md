# Google Shopping Ai Campaigns

## Run the Google Ads campaign types eligible to serve inside AI Mode and AI Overview — Performance Max, AI Max and Shopping — because AI Max matches the long conversational queries no manual keyword list can enumerate, and because final URL expansion means your collection and comparison pages become part of the targeting and must carry strong descriptive copy.

**Why it works:** Google is placing product ads inside the AI answer experience. AI Max reads query meaning plus behavioural signals rather than exact keywords. That lets it capture long-tail conversational searches that have no volume individually, and it dynamically rewrites headlines to fit the query context.

**Evidence offered:** The agency claims success running AI Max across a number of accounts and gives a pet-carrier query example sourced to Google. No figures are offered.

**Fit here (6/10):** This is the right channel eventually. It is blocked by money and signal volume, not by merit. PMax and AI Max both allocate budget against conversion signal. This account has ~54 orders/month, and the entire $30/day is committed to standing up the Meta giveaway campaign. The prerequisites are a second budget line, a Merchant Center feed and enough conversion events for Google's optimiser to read.

**Trigger:** Meta is at or under target CAC on its own budget, and a second ~$30/day plus a live Shopping feed exist.

**Staleness:** This is platform mechanics. The source article is dated 2026-08-21, so nothing here is stale yet. Platform mechanics age fast, though, so re-verify campaign-type eligibility against Google's own docs before spending on it.

**Do now:** The landing-page half is worth doing while the campaign waits. Put descriptive commercial copy on collection pages so final URL expansion has something to route to.

*Source: Jackson Blackledge (@blvckledge) — "How to get AI Overview & AI Mode to recommend your products | A Complete Guide" (social post)*

## Sequence Google behind Meta: hold it until Meta is working and the store does roughly $30–40K/month, then open a single Performance Max campaign capped at about 10% of total ad spend, run as a bottom-of-funnel layer that harvests the branded search Meta generates.
**Stage:** scale — parked until the scale phase opens.

**Why it works:** Meta creates demand, and people who see the ads then Google the brand. Google harvests demand that Meta made. Opening a second channel too early splits both budget and attention. One PMax campaign is the simplest way in, and the 10% cap keeps it a test that cannot starve the proven channel.

**Evidence offered:** The source asserts that "meta feeds Google really nicely" and calls PMax performance "really really great". No data is offered.

**Fit here (6/10):** The sequencing rule, revenue trigger and budget-share cap are durable. They add a scale-stage gate on top of the spend and conversion-volume floor in the campaign-type section above. The store is at about $2,700/month today against a trigger of about $30–40K/month, so this is parked behind scale.

**How to judge it:** Measure the Google layer by incremental Shopify branded-search revenue, not attributed ROAS. Amazon already captures branded demand, so attributed ROAS will overstate what Google adds.

**When the gate opens:** Re-check PMax as the entry type against Google's current campaign types, AI Max in particular.
