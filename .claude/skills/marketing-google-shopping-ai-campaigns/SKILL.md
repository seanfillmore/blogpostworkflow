# Google Shopping Ai Campaigns

## Run the Google Ads campaign types eligible to serve inside AI Mode and AI Overview — Performance Max, AI Max and Shopping — because AI Max matches the long conversational queries no manual keyword list can enumerate, and because final URL expansion means your collection and comparison pages become part of the targeting and must carry strong descriptive copy.

**Stage:** scale — parked until the scale phase opens. Recorded now so it is not re-derived later.

**Why it works:** Google is placing product ads inside the AI answer experience; AI Max reads query meaning plus behavioural signals rather than exact keywords, so it captures the long-tail conversational searches that individually have no volume, and dynamically rewrites headlines to the query context.

**Evidence offered:** Agency claims success running AI Max across a number of accounts; a pet-carrier query example sourced to Google; no figures.

**Fit here (6/10):** Right channel eventually, blocked purely by money and signal volume — not by merit. PMax and AI Max both allocate against conversion signal, and this account has ~54 orders/month with the entire $30/day committed to standing up the Meta giveaway campaign; a second budget line, a Merchant Center feed and enough conversion events for Google's optimiser to read are all prerequisites. Trigger: Meta is at or under target CAC on its own budget and a second ~$30/day plus a live Shopping feed exist. Platform mechanics class, and the source article is dated 2026-08-21 so nothing here is stale — but platform mechanics age fast, so re-verify the campaign-type eligibility against Google's own docs before spending on it. The landing-page half — descriptive commercial copy on collection pages so final URL expansion has something to route to — is worth doing while parked.
