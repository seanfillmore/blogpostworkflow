# Static Creative Formats

## Show the transformation visually rather than describing the after-state; before/after creative is legal, with tighter restrictions only on cosmetic procedures and weight loss.

**Why it works:** Problem-aware and solution-aware buyers are shopping for proof, not description; a visible before/after is the fastest proof format and it is the biggest gap in most ad accounts.

**Evidence offered:** Creator's account of ad-account gaps; explicit compliance correction that before/after is not banned in 2026.

**Fit here (6/10):** Durable format principle. Feasible for a solo operator with a phone camera — pit stains on a white shirt, chapped-to-healed lips, stained-to-clean teeth — and usable as Amazon listing images and PDP proof blocks without any ad spend. Scored mid because the compliance nuance mentioned is platform policy (fast-decay class) and marketplace image rules are stricter than the video implies.

## Always be testing a fixed library of static formats: educational infographic, headliner, benefits call-out, comparison (us vs them), transformation, grid, and text-only.

**Why it works:** Each format survives a different kind of copy and carries a different job, so a format library forces coverage across awareness levels instead of repeating one archetype; several formats trace back to Ogilvy-era print and still work.

**Evidence offered:** Creator's account of top performers (Laura Geller grid) plus the explicit print-era lineage; assertion otherwise.

**Fit here (6/10):** Durable principle class — the creator herself dates these to the print era, so age is irrelevant. Repurposable as Amazon listing image slots and organic posts: the grid format is explicitly recommended for multi-SKU brands, and 12 SKUs makes it a natural bundle/collection asset. Scored mid because 'always be testing' implies a test volume this account cannot fund; adopt the format library, not the testing cadence.
