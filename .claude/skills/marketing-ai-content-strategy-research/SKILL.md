# Ai Content Strategy Research

## Before asking an LLM for any content strategy, pipe real social data into it — your own post-level performance plus your competitors' top-performing videos with transcripts — because without that context the model is guessing and produces generic slop.

**Why it works:** The model has no knowledge of what is actually working in your niche, so it falls back on training priors and generic advice. Feeding it deep-analyzed real posts (what got views, what the hooks were, what topics are covered) turns generic content advice into recommendations specific to the niche's live behaviour.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion, plus the creator's claim that this data loop is why he has grown faster than most and why the business owners he coaches are 'crushing in their niche'. No numbers on the specific claim.

**Fit here (8/10):** The solo operator already films, edits and publishes RSC's own short-form video, so the planning input for that surface is a live surface today. He also already uses LLMs across the business, so the failure mode being described — briefing a model cold and getting slop — is one he will actually hit. The paid MCP tool is not required to run the principle: the scaled-down version is pasting the transcripts/captions of 15-20 top natural-deodorant and body-care short-form posts plus his own post-level view counts into the chat before asking for topic or hook ideas. Runnable today at $0.

## Have the LLM interview you through a fixed positioning framework before it writes anything — what you actually sell and who pays you, one core viewer described in full (demographics, psychographics, beliefs, what they have already tried and spent), their dream outcome, their pain points, a one-sentence authority statement and your unfair advantage — and answer each with extreme depth, because that brief is what every downstream topic, hook and script is targeted from.

**Why it works:** Content targeting quality is a function of the specificity of the input. A shallow avatar description yields generic hooks; a brief that names the exact prior spend, the exact fear and the exact self-talk gives the model concrete psychology to write hooks and scripts against, and it ripples through every later step of the workflow.

**Evidence offered:** Demonstrated live on the invented plastic-surgeon persona — the creator shows his own multi-paragraph answers and says 'the more you give, the better the targeting will be'. Assertion otherwise.

**Fit here (7/10):** One person can sit and answer these in 30 minutes, and the artifact — a persisted positioning brief — is reusable across every surface he writes (video scripts, Meta primary text, Amazon bullets, email). The honest translation for RSC is to derive the answers from actual repeat-buyer order history and existing reviews rather than inventing a persona, which keeps it consistent with the existing rule about deriving the narrowed profile from lowest-churning customers. Not a duplicate of the awareness-level claims: those govern who to aim at, this is the reusable written input document and the elicitation method.

## Differentiation inside a content niche comes from exactly seven attributes — topic selection, substance depth, unique stories/proof, avatar specificity, delivery style, storytelling format, visual format — so map the competitors you already listed across those seven, find where the niche is crowded and where it is empty, and combine two or more empty ones into your own lane.

**Why it works:** In a crowded category, undifferentiated content competes head-on with accounts that already have distribution. Locating a white-space combination the incumbents are not occupying means the audience has no substitute for your posts, so a new account grows faster on the same content quality.

**Evidence offered:** Assertion plus a worked example (facial-only plastic surgery serving 45-65 women as an underserved avatar slice versus the 25-40 nose-job content that dominates the niche). The 'positioning wheel' visualization is presented as output, not as proof.

**Fit here (7/10):** Durable positioning principle — slow decay, age irrelevant — and it applies directly to a 12-SKU natural deodorant and body-care brand posting its own video into a category saturated with clean-beauty content. The solo operator can do the mapping himself against a dozen competitor accounts in an afternoon; the plausible white space (substance depth on the transition/detox period, avatar specificity, founder-made proof) is exactly the kind of lane one person can hold. No existing skill records the seven-attribute inventory.

## Subscribe to a social-media data layer (Sandcastles.ai) and connect it to Claude through an MCP plugin, so the model can auto-build a competitor watch list, deep-analyze the top performers on every one of those channels, pull transcripts, and write the list back into the tool without you doing the collection by hand.

**Stage:** scale — parked until the scale phase opens. Recorded now so it is not re-derived later.

**Why it works:** The bottleneck in evidence-grounded content planning is data collection. An MCP bridge lets the LLM query a maintained index of hundreds of thousands of creator accounts and their post-level performance, so competitor mapping that would take days of manual scrolling happens inside one prompt, and the analysis stays connected to fresh data instead of a stale one-off export.

**Evidence offered:** Demonstrated live — Claude proposes eight tier-A and several tier-B accounts, creates the watch list inside the Sandcastles web app automatically, and produces the positioning wheel from deep-analyzed top performers. Creator asserts this loop is the reason for his growth.

**Fit here (6/10):** This is a paid analytics subscription, which the stage rules put behind `scale`. Against ~$2,700/mo combined revenue and a $30/day Meta budget, another monthly SaaS line for competitor social analytics is not yet the best marginal dollar, and the manual version of the same job covers the 20-account case fine. Trigger: he is posting short-form consistently, has already exhausted the manual competitor pull as his planning input, and organic is producing measurable Shopify sessions or list signups — then the subscription buys back real hours. Platform-mechanics class, so re-verify the tool and MCP mechanics before acting; specific product names decay fast.
