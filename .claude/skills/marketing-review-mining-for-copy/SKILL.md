# Review Mining For Copy

## Export your reviews to a CSV, hand them to an LLM, and have it surface 'golden nugget' testimonials to use as ad and page copy.

**Why it works:** Customers already articulate the benefit in the vocabulary and objection frame that converts other customers; borrowing their words beats writing new copy because it is pre-validated language.

**Evidence offered:** Creator states this has produced winners 'again and again' across her accounts.

**Fit here (8/10):** Durable principle, and the only input required is the existing Amazon and Shopify review corpus plus an LLM — no staff, no budget, no traffic. It does not need thousands of reviews to work; a few dozen produce usable language. Feeds CRO copy, the stage that gates paid spend.
