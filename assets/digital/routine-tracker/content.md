## Why 90 days?

Your skin's outer layer renews on roughly a 28-day cycle. Most dermatologists say to give a new routine 6–12 weeks before judging it. The most common reason a routine fails isn't the product — it's that people run out and stop before their skin catches up. Your reset is sized so that never happens.

## Your daily routine

Two steps. Consistency beats complexity every time.

### Every morning — Body Lotion

Apply to clean, dry skin after your shower or whenever skin feels tight. Massage in fully; it should absorb within minutes with no sticky film.

### Every night — Body Cream

Warm a small amount between your fingers and apply to dry patches before bed — elbows, knees, hands, feet. A little goes a long way, so one jar lasts the full 90 days.

:::callout title="The one rule that matters"
Apply within 3 minutes of getting out of the shower, while skin is still slightly damp. Damp skin holds moisture, and this single habit does more than any extra product ever could.
:::

## Your 12-week tracker

Tick each day you complete both steps. Rate your skin 1–5 at the end of each week — 1 is tight and flaky, 5 is comfortable all day. The pattern matters more than any single day.
