Thicker than the lotion — a cream for the places that need more. Reviewers reach for it on hands, knees, legs and feet, and a little goes further than you expect.

"Incredibly soft. Doesn't make you feel greasy or sticky after use. It makes you feel incredibly moisturized." — verified review

  • Thick, but it does not sit on you. It goes on like butter and works in — heavy enough for rough spots, without the slick film.
  • A little goes a long way. A tiny bit covers more than a pump of lotion does.
  • The scent is light. It comes from the oils, not added fragrance. Five to choose from above — and Pure Unscented is the same cream with none at all.
  • Beeswax is what makes it a cream. No petrolatum, no mineral oil, no synthetic fragrance, no parabens.

Two things worth knowing: warm a small amount between your fingers first — beeswax firms in cold weather. And because it is real coconut oil, it can separate a little in the jar.
