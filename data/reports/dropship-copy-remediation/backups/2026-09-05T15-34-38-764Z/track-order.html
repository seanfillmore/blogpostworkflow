Once your order is processed, an email will arrive with your tracking number (typically 2 to 5 days after making your purchase). Please insert your tracking number here.

Things to keep in mind:

Tracking numbers are not immediately up-to-date with tracking information. If you just got your tracking number, it should take 48 to 72 hours for the tracking information to appear. During that time it is normal for your package to appear as "not found", "information not available" or similar messages.

If more than 5 days have passed and your package still appears as not found, please contact us and we will be happy to help you.
