Toothpaste shouldn't trigger the gums it's meant to protect. Fresh Mint is built around three things — cold-pressed virgin coconut oil, aluminum-free baking soda and wildcrafted myrrh — not foam, not punch-in-the-face flavor, not paste-white pigment. The mint is essential-oil mild: organic peppermint and spearmint, not synthetic flavor amplifiers. If you're used to industrial toothpaste, the first few brushings will feel quiet. Many customers come to prefer it.

  • What is in it. Cold-pressed virgin coconut oil, unrefined, so it keeps everything a lab would take out.
  • Softer than your teeth. Aluminum-free baking soda polishes gently, instead of the cheap grit that is harder than your teeth.
  • No coating. Xanthan gum gives it body, without the film conventional paste leaves behind.
  • No SLS, no fluoride, no titanium dioxide, no synthetic sweeteners. Stevia sweetens it.

This is a gel, not a paste. Use it on a dry brush — there is no SLS in it, so it does not need water, and wetting the brush only thins the gel and it runs off. Shake before use; coconut oil firms up below 76°F.
