What currency are your prices in?
All prices are in USD.

When will my order ship?
Most orders leave us within 1–2 business days, by USPS or UPS. You’ll get a tracking number by email as soon as your parcel is on its way. If you haven’t received tracking within 3 business days, email support@realskincare.com with your order number.

How long does delivery take?
Most US orders arrive within about a week of shipping. Standard shipping is free on US orders over $45.

Can I return my order?
Yes. We have a 30-day return policy, and if anything arrives damaged, defective or simply not what you ordered, tell us straight away and we’ll make it right. Email support@realskincare.com with your order number to start a return — full details are on our refund policy page.

Can I cancel my order?
Yes, with no penalty, as long as it hasn’t shipped yet. Because we usually ship within a day, email support@realskincare.com as soon as you can. If it has already gone out, send it back under our return policy for a full refund.

I entered the wrong address — what now?
Email support@realskincare.com with your order number the moment you notice. We can correct an address within 24 hours of the order being placed, and orders often ship the next day, so it is worth telling us quickly.

I have a question that isn’t answered here.
Email support@realskincare.com and a real person will get back to you. Including your order number and a clear description of the problem gets you a faster answer.
