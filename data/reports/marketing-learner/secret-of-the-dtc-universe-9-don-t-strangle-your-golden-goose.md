# Secret of the DTC Universe #9: Don't strangle your golden goose

**Creator:** Stefan Georgi  
**Source:** social post — `secret-of-the-dtc-universe-9-don-t-strangle-your-golden-goose`  
**Published:** unknown (not supplied via --published)  
**Inferred era cues:** No explicit year. Era cues are advertorial-to-PDP funnel structure, ROAS/CAC/CPM/CPC vocabulary and the coinage 'AOVMaxx', which place it in the 2023–2026 DTC ecosystem. The content is durable-principle rather than platform-mechanic, so dating barely affects it.  

A short post arguing that when an acquisition funnel is already working, the correct action is no action. Georgi recounts advising a founder who scaled a new eCom brand to $130k in month three at a $30 CAC and 2.5x ROAS, then stalled only because he ran out of inventory. Asked to critique the copy, offer, mechanism and ad formats, Georgi told him to change nothing — the constraint was inventory, not creative, and tinkering with a working system mostly increases the odds of breaking it. He names the emotional drivers of unnecessary tinkering (guilt, insecurity, love of fiddling) and closes with the counterpoint: advertising is entropy, so the wall will come — CAC rises, margins compress, conversion rate drops, CTR falls while CPM and CPC climb — and that is when testing is licensed, not before.

Found 4 tactics: 3 adopted, 1 rejected.

## Adopted

### When the funnel and ads are already hitting target, change nothing — do not rewrite the headline, restructure the offer, build new advertorials, or test new ad formats. — 6/10

**Why it works:** A working system is a configuration you cannot yet explain; every change is an unattributed variable that can break it, so the expected value of tinkering on a winner is negative. Restraint preserves the thing producing money until an external signal says it has stopped.

**Evidence:** One anecdote: a founder at $130k revenue in month three, $30 CAC, 2.5x ROAS, throttled only by inventory. No before/after, no counterfactual, no figures on what tinkering costs.

**Fit:** Durable-principle class (decision discipline), so age is irrelevant. Directly live: a $30/day hand-run Meta campaign is being stood up, and one operator checking it daily is exactly the person who will rewrite the PDP hero and swap creative in week one because doing nothing feels like negligence. Held at 6 because it substantially overlaps restraint rules already recorded — the two-question triage in marketing-paid-media-measurement, the learning-phase freeze in marketing-paid-campaign-structure, and the doom-cycle rule in marketing-paid-creative-testing. The additive part is narrow but real: those rules govern the ad account, and this extends the same restraint to the *funnel assets* (PDP, offer structure, mechanism copy) when acquisition is already performing. Also worth stating honestly that RSC is not the case in the anecdote — nothing here is at 2.5x ROAS yet, so the rule mostly applies prospectively, and at ~54 orders/month 'it is working' is a directional judgement, not a measurement.

**Target skill:** `marketing-paid-media-measurement` (edit)

### Before critiquing copy, offers or creative, identify which constraint is actually binding — his was inventory, so the only correct next action was restocking and resuming spend. — 6/10

**Why it works:** Effort spent on a non-binding input produces no output. If the system is capped by something operational, every hour of creative or offer work is wasted regardless of how good the work is, because the cap does not move. Naming the binding constraint first is what makes the subsequent effort allocation correct.

**Evidence:** Single consulting-call anecdote where the founder asked four creative questions and the real answer was 'get inventory'. Assertion, no data.

**Fit:** Durable-principle class (diagnosis before action), age irrelevant, and it restates the standing operating reality in a usable procedural form: retention is the binding constraint here, not traffic or copy, so hours spent rewriting an Amazon bullet for the fourth time are hours not spent on the post-purchase and reorder flows where the 18–22.5% repeat rate is decided. Free, solo-executable, and it also names a real operational risk for a one-person catalog — a stockout on the deodorant SKU caps everything downstream and no marketing work fixes it. Held at 6 because it is a prioritisation rule rather than a revenue mechanism, it partly overlaps the allowable-CPL diagnostic ordering already in marketing-paid-campaign-structure, and the evidence is one anecdote.

**Target skill:** `marketing-performance-pattern-analysis` (edit)

### Pre-commit to the specific degradation signals that end the do-nothing period — rising CAC, compressing margins, falling conversion rate, falling CTR alongside rising CPM and CPC — because advertising decays and the wall will come. — 5/10

**Why it works:** Restraint without a defined exit condition becomes paralysis, and restraint without a defined trigger collapses back into tinkering the first uncomfortable day. Naming the metrics that license testing in advance means the decision to act is made against a stated condition rather than against how the operator feels on a given Tuesday, and framing decay as expected entropy stops a normal decline being read as a crisis.

**Evidence:** Assertion only — a list of four possible walls, with 'advertising is entropy, nothing gold can stay' as the reasoning. No thresholds, no account data.

**Fit:** Durable-principle class (decision rules), age irrelevant, and free to write down before the giveaway campaign has run a week. It is the missing counterpart to the restraint rules already recorded — those say do not touch it, this says what has to happen before you may. Capped at 5 because the signals are unquantified, it overlaps the frequency-exhaustion alarm and the two-question triage already in marketing-paid-media-measurement, and at $30/day driving giveaway entries the two metrics that actually matter here are cost per entry and entry-to-purchase rate, not ROAS and margin — so the list has to be rewritten in this campaign's terms, and every reading of it will be directional rather than significant at ~54 orders/month.

**Target skill:** `marketing-paid-media-measurement` (edit)

## Rejected

### Recognise that the urge to tinker comes from guilt, insecurity or a love of fiddling, and if you are a chronic tinkerer accept that you would rather tinker than get rich. — 2/10

**Rejected because:** Motivational framing with no actionable mechanism, and its only usable content duplicates the restraint tactic already adopted from this same post.

**Fit reasoning:** Motivational framing with no stated mechanism — there is no artifact to build, no surface to change, and no way to tell whether it was followed. The operational half of the same point (do nothing when something is working, and define the trigger that ends the freeze) is already captured in the two adopted entries above, so recording this separately would only add a near-duplicate that degrades skill triggering.
