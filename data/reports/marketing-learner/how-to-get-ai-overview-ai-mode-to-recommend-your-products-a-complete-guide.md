# How to get AI Overview & AI Mode to recommend your products | A Complete Guide

**Creator:** Jackson Blackledge (@blvckledge)  
**Source:** social post — `how-to-get-ai-overview-ai-mode-to-recommend-your-products-a-complete-guide`  
**Published:** 2026-08-21  
**Inferred era cues:** Published 2026-08-21. Names Google AI Overview and AI Mode, Gemini as the underlying model, a 'generative AI' section appearing in Google Search Console, Merchant Center 'conversational attributes' (question_and_answer up to 30 per product, document_link, related_product, item_group_title + variant_option, popularity_rank), AI Max and Performance Max campaign types with final URL expansion, and a distribution-shift timeline referencing TikTok Shop in 2022. Platform mechanics here are current, not stale.  

A Google Ads agency owner argues that Google's AI Overview and AI Mode are approaching ChatGPT in AI-driven ecommerce traffic and that most brands are ignoring them. He gives a six-part playbook: restructure existing pages so answers are stated up front, add FAQ and product schema, build dedicated 'for AI' pages (brand fact pages, certification pages, FAQ pages), publish listicle/roundup/comparison content on your own site and on third-party domains, earn independent third-party validation (heavily weighted toward Reddit, via aged multi-account seeding), clean and enrich the Google Merchant Center product feed including the new 'conversational attributes' (question_and_answer, document_link, related_product, item_group_title/variant_option, popularity_rank), and finally test Google Ads campaign types eligible to serve inside AI Mode — Performance Max, AI Max and Shopping — noting that AI Max's final-URL expansion makes every landing page part of the targeting. It ends with a pitch to his agency.

Found 13 tactics: 4 adopted, 9 rejected (1 of the adopted parked behind a stage gate).

## Adopted

### Get your brand written into independent third-party sources — review sites, comparison articles on external domains, industry publications, and roundups placed via outreach and guest posts — because a conclusion on someone else's domain carries more weight with AI than the same conclusion on yours. — 6/10

**Why it works:** AI answer engines look for validation from sources the brand does not control before recommending it, so third-party mentions raise the trust rung that on-site work cannot touch.

**Evidence:** Assertion, plus a screenshot claim that Reddit and third-party sources are the top sources Google pulls from for category and comparison questions.

**Fit:** This is the one item in the video that attacks the gap the fleet's own off-site test names: if a model ignored our domain entirely, nothing on the web would shortlist a $2,700/mo deodorant brand. Runnable today by the solo operator with no budget — a scaled-down version is emailing 15-25 natural-deodorant and clean-beauty bloggers/reviewers offering free product for an honest inclusion in their existing roundups, which is the same seeding motion marketing-pre-launch-product-validation already uses, pointed at publishers instead of buyers. It also produces reusable third-party proof for the PDP, so it earns its keep even if AI citation never arrives. Not scored higher because retention, not discovery, is the binding constraint on revenue.

**Target skill:** `marketing-ai-search-visibility` (edit)

### Run the Google Ads campaign types eligible to serve inside AI Mode and AI Overview — Performance Max, AI Max and Shopping — because AI Max matches the long conversational queries no manual keyword list can enumerate, and because final URL expansion means your collection and comparison pages become part of the targeting and must carry strong descriptive copy. — 6/10 · parked until `scale`

**Why it works:** Google is placing product ads inside the AI answer experience; AI Max reads query meaning plus behavioural signals rather than exact keywords, so it captures the long-tail conversational searches that individually have no volume, and dynamically rewrites headlines to the query context.

**Evidence:** Agency claims success running AI Max across a number of accounts; a pet-carrier query example sourced to Google; no figures.

**Fit:** Right channel eventually, blocked purely by money and signal volume — not by merit. PMax and AI Max both allocate against conversion signal, and this account has ~54 orders/month with the entire $30/day committed to standing up the Meta giveaway campaign; a second budget line, a Merchant Center feed and enough conversion events for Google's optimiser to read are all prerequisites. Trigger: Meta is at or under target CAC on its own budget and a second ~$30/day plus a live Shopping feed exist. Platform mechanics class, but the video is days old so nothing here is stale. The landing-page half — descriptive commercial copy on collection pages so final URL expansion has something to route to — is worth doing while parked.

**Target skill:** `marketing-google-shopping-ai-campaigns` (create)

### Check Google Search Console for its new generative-AI performance section, which reports AI-surface impressions and clicks separately. — 6/10

**Why it works:** Google is shipping dedicated reporting for AI Mode / AI Overview traffic, giving direct measurement of a channel that otherwise arrives disguised as branded search or direct.

**Evidence:** Mentioned in passing as evidence of Google's shipping speed ('in some accounts'), not as an instruction; no walkthrough.

**Fit:** Free, takes minutes, and needs no volume, budget or people — GSC is already connected to any Shopify store. It directly sharpens an existing recorded claim ('Expect standard analytics to under-report the AI touch… read the referral channel as a floor') by naming a first-party surface that measures AI impressions instead of inferring them, which is the difference between continuing and killing any AI-visibility spend. Scored 6 not higher because it is measurement, not revenue, and the prime directive here is revenue; it earns its place only as the stop/continue instrument on the other AI work. Platform mechanics class, rollout is partial ('in some accounts'), so confirm the section exists before planning around it.

**Target skill:** `marketing-ai-search-visibility` (edit)

### Find the questions buyers actually ask AI about your category, then restructure existing pages so each answer is stated cleanly and self-containedly up front rather than buried or spread across sections. — 5/10

**Why it works:** AI answer engines extract passages that immediately and unambiguously answer a query; a passage that needs context from elsewhere on the page to make sense loses to a source that states the answer outright.

**Evidence:** Assertion from a year of agency testing; no numbers, no named accounts.

**Fit:** Runnable today by one person on 12 SKUs — 'does natural deodorant actually work', 'why does baking soda sting', 'is it safe during pregnancy' are known category questions and answering each in a self-contained on-page block costs an afternoon. Scored mid rather than high because the existing ai-search-visibility off-site test applies squarely: at ~$2,700/mo combined revenue the web barely mentions this brand at all, so comprehension fixes on our own domain do not move the trust rung, and the prime directive is revenue rather than citations. Platform mechanics class, but the video is days old so age is not the discount.

**Target skill:** `marketing-ai-search-visibility` (edit)

## Rejected

### Build new pages whose only job is to feed AI models — a brand fact page as a single source of truth, certification/classification pages, and FAQ pages with neutral 3-5 sentence answers — accepting that most human visitors will never land on them. — 3/10

**Rejected because:** Duplicates (as its negation) the existing marketing-ai-search-visibility claim 'Do not write separate content for AI and do not chunk pages into small fragments for extraction… because the fragment-generating pattern is the one Google names as scaled content abuse', and the FAQ component duplicates the journey-staged FAQ claim in marketing-conversion-friction-audit.

**Fit reasoning:** Collides head-on with a claim the fleet already holds in marketing-ai-search-visibility: 'Do not write separate content "for AI" and do not chunk pages into small fragments for extraction — write for people and organise with ordinary headings, because the fragment-generating pattern is the one Google names as scaled content abuse.' The FAQ-page half additionally duplicates the journey-staged FAQ module already specified in marketing-conversion-friction-audit. Adopting the inverse of a recorded rule would degrade the skill rather than extend it.

### Publish listicle, roundup and 'best [category] for [use case]' pages on your own site that compare multiple products on price, features, who each suits, key differences and limitations, positioning your product inside the consideration set. — 3/10

**Rejected because:** Duplicate of the existing marketing-ai-search-visibility claim 'Do not publish a self-ranked best [category] guide expecting it to win the recommendation… your own buyer's guide reliably earns the citation while the answer recommends someone else.'

**Fit reasoning:** Restates a claim already recorded and already qualified in marketing-ai-search-visibility: 'Do not publish a self-ranked "best [category]" guide expecting it to win the recommendation — for a brand that is not already the consensus pick, your own buyer's guide reliably earns the citation while the answer recommends someone else.' The same skill also records that after the August 2026 ChatGPT retrieval change, listicle and comparison citations fell by half and a third. A 12-SKU brand at ~54 orders/month is exactly the not-yet-consensus case that claim was written about.

### Enrich the Merchant Center feed with Google's new conversational attributes — question_and_answer (up to 30 per product), document_link, related_product, item_group_title + variant_option, popularity_rank — via supplemental feeds for a small catalog, starting with bestsellers. — 3/10

**Rejected because:** Near-variant of the already-failed 'Make product data complete and machine-readable — fill the taxonomy, attributes and structured fields — so AI buying agents can parse the catalogue and rank the product into their recommendations'; conversational attributes are the same bet with newer field names.

**Fit reasoning:** This is the same failed tactic at a finer grain — filling structured product fields so AI buying agents can parse and rank the catalogue — and it failed here already. It also presumes a Google Merchant Center feed and Shopping presence this business does not maintain; the whole $900/mo paid budget is committed to the Meta giveaway campaign. Platform mechanics class and genuinely current (August 2026), but currency does not rescue a mechanism already disproven on this account.

### Diagnose invisibility in AI answers as one of exactly two problems: there is not enough information about you on the web, or the information that exists is hard for machines to parse (buried in images/PDFs, or simply inaccurate). — 3/10

**Rejected because:** Duplicate, and a weaker version, of the existing marketing-ai-search-visibility claim splitting an AI-visibility complaint into technical, comprehension or trust causes.

**Fit reasoning:** This is a coarser version of a diagnostic the fleet already holds in marketing-ai-search-visibility: 'split the complaint into one of three causes — technical (we cannot be crawled or parsed), comprehension (we are read but described inaccurately or vaguely), or trust (we are understood and simply not selected)'. His two causes collapse into technical and comprehension and drop the trust rung entirely, which for a brand this small is the one that actually binds. Recording it would blunt the existing claim.

### Publish direct '[product] vs [competitor]' comparison pages where there is genuine search demand. — 3/10

**Rejected because:** Duplicate of the existing marketing-conversion-friction-audit claim about hosting competitor comparisons on your own page, plus the us-vs-them format claims already held in marketing-product-image-stack and marketing-conversion-copy-angles.

**Fit reasoning:** Duplicates the recorded claim in marketing-conversion-friction-audit: 'host the comparison and third-party studies on your own page so the buyer never has to leave to verify you', and the us-vs-them format is already prescribed in marketing-product-image-stack and marketing-conversion-copy-angles. The creator's own precondition also fails here — a brand doing ~$2,700/mo has effectively no branded search volume for a '[us] vs [them]' query to capture.

**Fit reasoning:** Directly restates a tactic already tested here and failed: 'Emit FAQPage and HowTo structured data so AI engines can extract question-and-answer pairs and process steps directly from the page.' The product-schema half is the same mechanism as the second failed tactic about machine-readable structured product fields.

### Build multiple Reddit accounts over time, farm karma with unrelated participation, then start dropping brand mentions into product-category threads once the accounts have history. — 2/10

**Rejected because:** The honest residue duplicates marketing-acquisition-channel-selection's claim about posting into the communities where your buyers already congregate; the delta is multi-account astroturfing, which is a platform ToS violation with no honest translation.

**Fit reasoning:** Multi-account brand seeding is explicit Reddit ToS violation and vote/identity manipulation — one ban wipes the asset and the brand risk lands on a solo operator with no PR cover. The legitimate version, participating openly where buyers already are, duplicates the recorded claim in marketing-acquisition-channel-selection: 'Post your customers' success stories into the online groups and communities where your buyers already congregate, as a standing lead source alongside ads.' Nothing is left after removing the sockpuppet layer.

### Clean up the product feed — accurate titles, descriptions, images, identifiers, reviews, sizes and colours — because the feed is no longer just a Google Ads asset and AI will skip or misreport a product with vague or outdated attributes. — 2/10

**Rejected because:** Already tested here and failed — restates 'Make product data complete and machine-readable — fill the taxonomy, attributes and structured fields — so AI buying agents can parse the catalogue and rank the product into their recommendations.'

**Fit reasoning:** Near-variant of a tactic already tested here and failed: 'Make product data complete and machine-readable — fill the taxonomy, attributes and structured fields — so AI buying agents can parse the catalogue and rank the product into their recommendations.' Same mechanism, same expected payoff, just scoped to the Merchant Center feed.

### Build your own 'independent' review sites so there is a wider pool of credible-looking third-party sources for LLMs to pull from. — 1/10

**Rejected because:** No honest translation at any size: presenting a site you own as an independent reviewer is undisclosed self-endorsement and a documented penalty pattern; the legitimate version duplicates the third-party-placement tactic already adopted from this same video.

**Fit reasoning:** Astroturf: a self-owned site presented as independent is undisclosed self-endorsement and the scaled-content/link-scheme pattern search engines penalise, and a solo operator maintaining satellite domains at ~54 orders/month is pure overhead with no revenue path. The honest version — earning mentions on sites you do not own — is already adopted above, so this adds only the deceptive delta.
