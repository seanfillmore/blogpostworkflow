# Soap Giveaway — Meta creative briefs

Campaign `120248756571100173` · Ad set `120248756573810173` · $30/day · US · 25–60 · optimizing `LEAD`

**The prize:** 36 bars of Pure Unscented Moisturizing Coconut Soap over 3 years (3 shipments/year, 4 bars each) plus 3 Sensitive Skin Moisturizing Sets, one per year. ARV **$536.40**. **Two winners** — the drawn entrant *and* the person who referred them.

**The one goal of every asset here is an ENTRY, not a sale.** Nothing in these frames sells a product, quotes a price, or links to a PDP. The conversion event is `Lead`, fired on entry.

---

## Rules every asset must pass

1. **One job, one persona per frame.** Do not merge two of these briefs to save a render.
2. **The headline is the focal point**, then the key visual, then everything else.
3. **1-second comprehension at phone size.** View the finished asset at actual phone dimensions before it ships. If the smallest text isn't readable at a glance, the copy is too long — cut it, don't shrink it.
4. **Claims live on the product, not stamped on the image.** "One ingredient" is true of the unscented bar and can be shown by the pack; do not overlay a claim the packaging doesn't make.
5. **The soap must look like the real soap.** Generated bars, labels and cartons that don't match the physical product are a compliance problem, not a style choice — use reference photos.

## ⚠️ Meta policy guardrails — these get ads rejected

- **No personal attributes.** Never "your eczema", "do you have sensitive skin?", "struggling with dry skin?" Meta reads second-person assertions about a health condition as claiming to know the viewer. Write about the *product* or *the giveaway*, not about the viewer's body.
- **No before/after skin imagery, and no implied medical outcome.** This is why there is deliberately **no transformation brief below**, even though transformation is normally the strongest format for problem-aware buyers. In this category on paid social it is the fastest route to a rejection and an account flag.
- **Sweepstakes must show the terms.** "No purchase necessary" and a rules link belong in the ad copy, not buried on the lander.
- Avoid "free" as the first word of the headline on frame 1 if rejections start — swap to "Win".

## Frame 1 — Headliner (broad, cold)

- **Job:** stop the scroll and state the prize. This is the volume driver.
- **Persona:** none in particular — deliberately broad. This is the only frame allowed to be generic.
- **Format:** headliner / text-forward.
- **Headline (the focal point, huge):** `3 YEARS OF SOAP.`
  Sub, roughly one-third the size: `36 bars. Two winners. Zero cost.`
- **Visual:** a single unscented bar, clean, centred low, on a plain warm-neutral background. Nothing else. The type does the work.
- **Do not include:** price, product range, ingredient list, a person, the $536.40 figure.
- **1-second test:** a stranger should be able to say "soap giveaway" and nothing more.

## Frame 2 — Us-vs-them (the Ingredient-Label Reader)

- **Job:** make the giveaway feel worth entering *because of what the soap is*.
- **Persona:** persona #1 in `personas.json` — reads the back of the pack, assumes "natural" is greenwashing.
- **Format:** us-vs-them comparison. **Cap at 4 rows.** Only these, because they are all true and all material:

| | Real Skin Care | Typical "natural" bar |
|---|---|---|
| Ingredients | **1** | 12–30 |
| Added fragrance | None | Usually, incl. "unscented" |
| Palm oil | None | Common |
| Packaging | Paper + box, no plastic | Plastic film |

- **Headline:** `ONE INGREDIENT.`
- **Visual:** left column the real bar with its paper wrap; right column a deliberately generic unbranded bar. **Never show a competitor's real brand.**
- **Copy anchor (VOC, verbatim-derived):** "Finally, a soap without anything added."
- **Do not include:** a named competitor, a health claim, more than 4 rows.

## Frame 3 — Lifestyle (the Fragrance-Sensitive buyer)

- **Job:** let the sensitive-skin buyer recognise themselves and enter.
- **Persona:** persona #3 — reacts to everything, has stopped trusting new products.
- **Format:** lifestyle / in-context.
- **Avatar:** a woman, 30s–40s, ordinary and un-styled — not a model.
- **Setting:** a real bathroom sink in daylight. Plain tile, a folded towel, the bar on a simple dish.
- **Emotional register:** calm and unremarkable. The feeling is *relief*, not delight. No laughing, no towel-on-head, no spa.
- **Headline:** `UNSCENTED. ACTUALLY.`
- **Copy anchor:** "It's not some fake perfumey fragrance."
- **Do not include:** second-person claims about skin, any before/after, visible redness or irritation.

## Frame 4 — What's in the box (broad)

- **Job:** make $536.40 tangible. Abstract value doesn't motivate an entry; a visible pile does.
- **Persona:** broad.
- **Format:** grid / benefit callout.
- **Visual:** all **36 bars** arranged in a clean grid or stack, with the 3 Sensitive Skin Sets alongside. This is the one frame where quantity is the message.
- **Headline:** `THIS IS THE PRIZE.`
  Sub: `36 bars + 3 sets. Delivered over 3 years.`
- **Do not include:** a person, a price, lifestyle staging.
- **1-second test:** the viewer should register "that is a lot of soap" before reading a word.

## Frame 5 — Two winners (the referral mechanic)

- **Job:** drive the +5 referral rung, which is the cheapest entry volume in the ladder.
- **Persona:** broad, but speaks to someone who has already entered — **best used as a retargeting frame**, not cold.
- **Format:** benefit callout, split composition.
- **Headline:** `YOU BOTH WIN.`
  Sub: `Refer a friend. If they win, you win the same prize.`
- **Visual:** two identical prize stacks side by side, mirrored. The symmetry *is* the idea.
- **Do not include:** anything implying a purchase improves odds — this is the single most dangerous claim in the whole campaign. Purchases earn nothing, and the rules say so.

## Ad copy (pairs with any frame)

**Primary text**

> Three years of soap. One ingredient — saponified coconut oil. Nothing else.
>
> We're giving away 36 bars and 3 Sensitive Skin Sets, delivered over three years. Two winners: whoever's drawn, and whoever referred them.
>
> Entering takes about ten seconds. No purchase necessary.

**Headline:** `Win 3 Years of Soap` · **Description:** `36 bars. Two winners. No purchase necessary.` · **CTA:** `Sign Up`

**Rotate the first line** per frame — frame 2 leads with "One ingredient", frame 3 with "Unscented, actually", frame 4 with "Three years of soap".

---

## Production order

Frames **1 and 4** first — they're broad, need no persona accuracy, and will carry most of the spend. Frames 2 and 3 are the differentiated follow-ups once something is delivering. Frame 5 only matters once there are entrants to retarget.
