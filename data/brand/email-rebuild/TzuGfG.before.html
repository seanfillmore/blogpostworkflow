Why this exists

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }} — I am Sean, co-founder. My sister-in-law Julie started Real Skin Care over 20 years ago, after looking for skincare that was both organic and genuinely simple and finding that it mostly did not exist.

Twenty years later the test we apply to every formula is still hers: if a cheaper substitute would do the same job, would we use it? The answer is yes, honestly — if it would. The reason we do not is that the cheap substitutes do not.

Baking soda is softer than enamel and neutralises oral acid. Hydrated silica is harder than enamel and simply abrades. Silica is cheaper. We use baking soda. That decision repeats itself across every product we make.
