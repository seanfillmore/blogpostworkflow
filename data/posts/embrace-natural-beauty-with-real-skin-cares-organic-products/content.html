What Makes a Product Truly Organic?

Organic skincare uses plant-derived ingredients grown without conventional pesticides or GMOs. Truly organic formulas skip synthetic fragrances, artificial colorants, and harsh fillers that can irritate your skin or the environment.

Why it matters:

  • Fewer irritants — no unnecessary chemicals or fillers
  • Better moisture support — fatty acids and plant oils help lock in hydration
  • Lower-impact sourcing — fewer synthetics from start to finish

FAQs

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. Our formulas skip common irritants like synthetic fragrance and harsh sulfates. If you're highly reactive, patch test first. See our guides on the best body lotion for sensitive skin and best natural deodorant for sensitive skin.

What's the difference between the Lotion and the Cream?

The Lotion is lightweight and fast-absorbing — good for daily use. The Cream is richer and better for very dry areas or colder months. Both are clean and simple.

Is this considered raw organic skincare?

Our products are formulated with certified organic, plant-derived ingredients and made without synthetic chemicals. We don't use the term "raw" because our formulas are crafted and blended — but the philosophy is the same: real ingredients, nothing artificial.

Do you use parabens, phthalates, or synthetic dyes?

No. None of our products contain parabens, phthalates, synthetic dyes, or petroleum-based ingredients.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes. We never test on animals.

How do I transition to a natural bodycare routine?

Start with a cleanser and moisturizer. Add deodorant and lip balm next. Keep it simple and consistent — that's it.
