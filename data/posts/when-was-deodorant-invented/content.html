Body Odor Before Deodorant

Managing body odor is not a modern idea.

  • Ancient Egypt (around 3000 BCE): People rubbed scented oils and pastes made from cinnamon, citrus, and other plants on their skin.
  • Ancient Rome: People used alum, a mineral salt, as an astringent. It is an aluminum compound, so the main ingredient in modern antiperspirants has been around for a very long time.
  • Medieval Europe: People tucked lavender and rosemary into their clothes. Changing linens was seen as cleaner than washing the body.

What changed in the late 1800s was that odor control became a product you could buy.

When Was Deodorant Invented? (1888)

The first commercial deodorant was Mum, patented in 1888 in Philadelphia. That answers "when was the first deodorant made" and "what year was deodorant invented." It is earlier than most people guess.

Mum was a zinc-based cream you applied with your fingertips. It targeted odor-causing bacteria but did not stop sweat.

Who Invented Deodorant?

No one knows for sure. Some sources credit an inventor with the initials "M.H.," but no full name has been confirmed. The product is better documented than the person who made it.

Where Was Deodorant Invented?

In the United States. Mum was made and patented in Philadelphia. Everything before 1888 was folk practice, and Mum was the first real product.

When Was Antiperspirant Invented? (1903)

A deodorant fights odor. An antiperspirant reduces sweat by blocking sweat ducts. They work in different ways.

The first commercial antiperspirant, Everdry, came out around 1903. Its active ingredient was aluminum chloride, a salt that temporarily plugs sweat ducts. Most antiperspirants still work this way. Early versions could irritate skin and damage fabric, and aluminum salts stayed the core ingredient through the 20th century.

That is what "aluminum-free" on a label is distancing itself from.

When Did Deodorant Become a Thing? How "Odor Shame" Sold It

For its first few decades, most Americans did not use deodorant. Body odor was a normal part of life.

Advertising changed that. Starting in the 1910s, Odorono ads told women their natural scent was embarrassing and could hurt their marriages and careers. A blunt 1919 campaign reportedly led some readers to cancel their magazine subscriptions, and Odorono's sales rose sharply anyway.

Deodorant was marketed almost only to women at first. Ads aimed at men picked up in the 1930s, and men's deodorant became a mainstream staple by the 1950s.

So the pressure many of us feel about body odor was largely built by marketing, not biology. It is one reason people now ask harder questions about what is in these products.

When Was Roll-On Deodorant Invented? (1952)

Roll-on deodorant came out in 1952 as Ban, made by Bristol-Myers. The idea came from the ballpoint pen: a rolling ball that spreads liquid cleanly and evenly. It was faster and neater than a cream.

The format never went away. It spreads liquid formulas evenly, wastes little, and works as well with natural ingredients as with conventional ones. See our best natural roll-on deodorant picks.

When Was Spray Deodorant Invented? (1960s)

Aerosol spray deodorants hit shelves in the mid-1960s and peaked in the 1970s. They felt futuristic: fast and hands-free.

Then came problems. Research linked CFC propellants to ozone damage, and the US banned CFCs in most aerosols in 1978. Concerns about breathing in aluminum compounds from sprays added to the drop. Modern pump sprays replaced the old versions, but the category never fully recovered.

When Was the Deodorant Stick Invented?

Solid sticks emerged in the mid-20th century and became the default format by the late 1970s. They are dry, fast, and need no technique: twist, swipe, done. For many people, "deodorant" came to mean "stick."

That is changing as roll-ons and natural formulas gain ground.

¿Cuándo Se Inventó el Desodorante?

El primer desodorante comercial se inventó en 1888: se llamaba Mum y se patentó en Filadelfia, Estados Unidos. No se sabe con certeza quién lo inventó. El primer antitranspirante llegó en 1903, el desodorante en roll-on se creó en 1952 y el aerosol apareció en los años 60.
