Why 90 days?

Your skin's outer layer renews on roughly a 28-day cycle. Most dermatologists say to give any new routine 6–12 weeks before you judge it. The #1 reason routines fail isn't the product — it's that people run out and stop before their skin catches up. Your reset is sized so that never happens.

Your daily routine

Two steps. That's it. Consistency beats complexity every time.

Every morning

Body Lotion

Apply to clean, dry skin after your shower or whenever skin feels tight. Massage in fully — it should absorb within minutes with no sticky film.

Lotion is your daily driver. You have three bottles for a reason — use it generously.

Every night

Body Cream

Warm a small amount between your fingers and apply to dry patches before bed — elbows, knees, hands, feet.

The cream is your repair layer. A little goes a long way, so one jar lasts the full 90 days.

The one rule that matters

Apply within 3 minutes of getting out of the shower, while skin is still slightly damp. Damp skin holds moisture; this single habit does more than any extra product ever could.

The plan

Your 90 days, in three phases

You don't have to think about this — just follow the routine. But knowing what's happening helps you stick with it when week 2 feels slow.

Weeks 1–2 · Reset

Clear the slate

Patch test first (see the field guide). Then start the two-step routine daily. The first win is often about what you stop putting on your skin — no fragrance, no harsh actives, no drugstore irritants. Expect skin to feel calmer and less tight almost immediately.

Weeks 3–6 · Rebuild

Barrier repair

This is where consistency pays off. Dry patches show up less often. Skin that used to react starts to settle. Keep both steps going every day — this is the stretch most people quit, and the stretch that matters most.

Weeks 7–12 · Maintain

Lock it in

Soft, resilient skin becomes the new normal. Notice how much less you think about it. By the end of your reset you'll know exactly how much product your skin wants — and whether it's time to set up a restock so you never slide backward.

Daily habits that speed things up

  • Shorter, cooler showers — hot water strips the barrier you're rebuilding
  • Pat dry, don't rub; leave skin slightly damp before lotion
  • Drink water and get sleep — skin repairs overnight
  • Reapply lotion to hands after every wash
