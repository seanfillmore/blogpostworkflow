## $100M Offers → the offer

**Core frameworks:** Two ways to grow (more customers × worth more; measure in **gross profit / LTGP**, not revenue). Commodity vs **Grand Slam Offer** (category of one — can't be price-compared). **Starving Crowd** (Market > Offer > Persuasion; 4 indicators: pain, purchasing power, easy to target, growing). **Premium pricing / virtuous cycle** (raise value then price; never race to cheapest). **The Value Equation = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)** — raise top two, *lower bottom two* (the pros attack the bottom). **Offer build:** Dream Outcome → list every Problem → reverse to Solutions → Delivery vehicles (Delivery Cube) → **Trim & Stack** (keep low-cost/high-value + high-cost/high-value; favor one-to-many). **Scarcity** (limited qty; honest/limited-batch, actually sell out & announce). **Urgency** (rolling cohorts, rolling seasonal wrappers, price/bonus deadline, exploding opportunity; ~50–60% of launch sales in last 4h). **Bonuses** (stack, name each with a benefit, ascribe $ value, total value eclipses core; never discount core; tools/checklists > trainings; can use other people's products). **Guarantees** ("if not X in Y, then Z"; unconditional best for low-ticket B2C; do the refund math — more sales at higher refund rate wins). **MAGIC naming** (Magnet, Avatar, Goal, Interval, Container).

**RSC application:** Reframe the $46.80 Set as a **named value stack (~$115 value)**, not a "% off" discount. Attack **Perceived Likelihood** (131 reviews, sensitive-skin proof) — cheapest lever on a 0.82% page. **Buy-X-Get-Y-Free** framing. Named guarantee ("Calm-Skin Promise," keep-the-product). Thin SKUs = **named bonuses on skin orders**, never front-line acquisition. Honest limited-batch seasonal drops + rolling seasonal wrappers.

**Do NOT:** take "charge 100× / be most expensive" literally on a consumable (CeraVe sets the ceiling); fake "only 3 left"; anti-guarantee the hero; lead with the markdown; demand empty-bottle returns to save $5 COGS; churn a winning offer (vary the wrapper, not the machine).

## $100M Leads → traffic

**Core frameworks:** **Lead** (can contact) vs **Engaged Lead** (shows interest — the real output). **Lead Magnet** = complete solution to a narrow problem that reveals the next problem your core offer solves (salty-pretzel logic; 7 steps; types: Reveal-Problem, Sample/Trial [named ideal for *consumables/cosmetics*], One-Step; delivered via software/info/service/physical). **The CORE FOUR** (Warm×Cold × 1:1×1:many): **#1 Warm Outreach** (list, 100/day, A-C-A, Value Equation offer, "first five free," raise price as referrals start, 9-word email); **#2 Post Content** (audience is the asset; **Hook→Retain→Reward**; give:ask ~4:1; "give until they ask"; "How to"→"How I"; niche Puddles→Oceans); **#3 Cold Outreach** (list → big fast value → volume; slow, ~1yr, team-heavy); **#4 Paid Ads** (Call-Out → Value [What/Who/When] → CTA; **Track/Lose/Print money**; budget 2× a customer's 30-day cash to test, kill at 1×; **LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1**; below-3× industry CAC = business-model problem, above = ad problem; **CFA**). **More/Better/New** (Rule of 100; test one thing/week; attack the constraint). **Lead Getters:** customer **referrals** (referrals − churn; two-sided incentive; ask at POS), employees (3 Ds), agencies (learn then in-house), **affiliates/partners** (Whisper-Tease-Shout; Integration).

**RSC application:** Prioritize **Content (agent fleet) > Paid ads (skin only) > Referrals (two-sided) > Affiliates (estheticians/sensitive-skin) > Warm (9-word to dormant list) > [skip cold D2C]**. **Free digital quiz** = cold-traffic lead magnet. **CFA is the linchpin** — Set + Zipify + Recurpay must return CAC+fulfillment in 30 days. At 0.82% CVR it's a **conversion problem, not an ad problem** → Track → CRO → LTGP → then scale (= the plan spine).

**Do NOT:** ship free physical samples to cold paid traffic (kills economics at this AOV); acquire on low-margin catalog; scale ads before tracking is trustworthy; run cold-outreach D2C solo; over-ask the content audience (kills the golden goose); jump to New before exhausting More/Better; neglect product (18% repeat = goodwill gap); expect a fast finish (5–10yr; 3–6mo/new channel).

## $100M Money Models → spend policy (most important for RSC)

**Core frameworks:** A **Money Model** = deliberate sequence of offers; build one stage at a time (Get Cash → Get More → Get Most). **The 30-day rule (CFA):** a good money model makes **more profit per customer than it costs to get+service them in 30 days** (breakeven); the infinite-scaling threshold is **one customer funds ~two more in <30 days (~2×)** — because a credit card floats you 30 days. Value×volume×velocity: 2×2×2 = 8× growth. **Four offer types:** **Attraction** (Win-Your-Money-Back [only if refunds <5%], Giveaway, Decoy, **Buy-X-Get-Y-Free** ["free" > "% off"; give more free than paid], Pay-Less-Now/More-Later); **Upsell** (Classic "can't have X without Y"; Menu [unsell→prescribe→A/B→card-on-file]; **Anchor** [show 5–10× price first]; **Rollover** [credit prior purchase, next offer ≥4× credit]); **Downsell** (Payment Plan; Trial-with-Penalty; **Feature/quantity** — never same thing cheaper); **Continuity** (front it with a cash offer — *never a standalone membership*; Continuity Bonus > first payment; Continuity Discount — **bill 4-week cycles = +8.3% rev**; Waived-Fee; Profitwell: monthly 10.7% cancel vs quarterly 5%; lifetime discount at churn point).

**RSC money model & CFA math (real numbers):**
- **Set as attraction:** 30-day GP ≈ **$22** → below breakeven vs $25 CAC (needs CAC <~$22).
- **$99 "90-Day Coconut Reset" bundle (contribution ~$47) as attraction:** 30-day GP ≈ **$50** → **~2× a $25 CAC = infinite-scaling threshold.**
- **Policy:** CAC ceiling = first-order contribution of the acquiring offer; run cheap offers at breakeven, push to 2× **only on the bundle**. Continuity (2nd charge ~day 42–56) is the LTV engine, not the CFA engine.

**Do NOT:** make the subscription the cold attraction offer; monthly-bill a 6–8wk-consumption product; lean on a flat 15%-off discount; drop price to close; run refund-heavy attraction offers on passive consumables; over-collect prepay cash you can't fulfill; run unvetted giveaways (regulated).
